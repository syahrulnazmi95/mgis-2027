Tracks and topics

We welcome papers on, but not limited to

Track 01

GeoAI methods and foundation models

  • Deep learning for satellite, aerial and UAV imagery
  • LiDAR and point cloud classification
  • Geospatial foundation and vision-language models
  • Agentic and conversational GeoAI
Track 02

Geospatial Knowledge Infrastructure

  • From SDI to GKI: frameworks and roadmaps
  • Data governance, sharing and sovereignty
  • Geospatial knowledge graphs and semantics
  • National mapping agency transformation
Track 03

Digital twins and smart cities

  • 3D city models and GeoBIM
  • Real-time sensing and IoT integration
  • Urban analytics and spatial decision support
  • Indoor and underground mapping
Track 04

Cadastre, land and boundaries

  • 3D and multipurpose cadastre
  • AI-assisted surveying and boundary detection
  • Geodetic infrastructure and GNSS
  • International boundary management
Track 05

Climate, disaster and environment

  • Flood, landslide and coastal hazard mapping
  • Agriculture and food security monitoring
  • Land cover change and carbon accounting
  • Early warning and emergency response
Track 06

Trust, ethics and capacity building

  • Data quality and uncertainty in GeoAI
  • Explainable and responsible GeoAI
  • Geospatial education and workforce skills
  • Policy, standards and legal frameworks

Review and ethics

  • Remove author names and affiliations from the review copy
  • Every paper goes through a plagiarism check
  • Reviewers declare any conflict of interest
  • Declare any use of generative AI in writing
