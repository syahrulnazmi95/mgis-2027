About the summit

Where AI meets national geospatial knowledge

Hosted by the Department of Survey and Mapping Malaysia (JUPEM), the summit brings together mapping agencies, researchers and industry from around the world to share how GeoAI is turning geospatial data into trusted, decision-ready knowledge.

Three days of keynotes, a leadership forum, technical paper sessions, hands-on workshops and an exhibition, with 2 CPD points for every registered participant who attends.

Call for papers

Six thematic tracks

Submit abstract by 15 Jan 2027
Track 01

GeoAI methods and foundation models

Deep learning for Earth observation, LiDAR and point clouds, and geospatial foundation models.

Track 02

Geospatial Knowledge Infrastructure

Policy, governance, data sharing and the move from SDI to GKI.

Track 03

Digital twins and smart cities

3D city models, GeoBIM and real-time spatial decision support.

Track 04

Cadastre, land and boundaries

Modern land administration, boundary management and AI-assisted surveying.

Track 05

Climate, disaster and environment

Flood, landslide and coastal intelligence for resilience planning.

Track 06

Trust, ethics and capacity building

Data quality, explainable GeoAI and growing the next generation of geospatial talent.
